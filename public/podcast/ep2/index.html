Vibe coding, and the rise of the product engineer

Abrar builds GradeDrive, an AI marking tool for schools, with a non-technical co-founder, and helps a second startup where the product team ships its own ideas. He explains what makes that safe: a skill that gives every idea its own worktree, database and dev server, a read-only environment the agent can be trusted with, guidelines written after a feature that ran a whole class's PDF in the browser, and a draft pull request that spins up an environment of its own. James asks about native apps, things going off the rails and what the job is now. The answers: web is easy and native is not, it is easier than ever to build sloppy stuff, taste still comes from people, and Abrar has not opened a code editor in six months.
