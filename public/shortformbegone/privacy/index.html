ShortformBeGone collects no data.

This is the privacy policy for the ShortformBeGone app for iPhone and iPad. Everything the app does happens on your device, and nothing leaves it.

01 — The app

What it sees, what it keeps and what it sends.

What the filter sees

To decide whether to block something, the content filter is shown the web address (in Safari and in-app browsers) or the hostname (for other apps' connections) of each new connection on your device. It compares that with the rule list on your device and answers "allow" or "block".

The Safari blocker

The app gives Safari a list of rules. Safari applies them itself; the app and its extension are never shown the pages or addresses you visit.

The Safari guard

A small script runs inside pages on youtube.com, instagram.com, facebook.com and snapchat.com only. It reads the address of the page, and if that address is a short-form section it takes you to the site's home page. Before leaving it pauses any video on that page and hides the page. It reads no page content, stores nothing and sends nothing.

What it keeps

Nothing. It keeps no history and writes no record of what you visit. If you switch on LogMatches in your own profile, the name of the rule that matched (for example youtube.com/shorts) is written to your device's system log; the address you visited is not.

What it sends

Nothing. The part of the filter that is shown addresses runs in a sandbox with no network access, and no part of it makes network connections. The app makes no network requests, has no account, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party code.

What the app stores

Which setup steps you have ticked, on your device only.

Because nothing is collected, there is nothing to share, sell, or delete.

02 — This website

The page you are reading is a separate matter from the app, and it is covered elsewhere.

The app never contacts agentsee.work. You reach this site only by opening it yourself, for instance to download the profile files on your Mac. The site sets no cookies and runs no analytics; what its host sees of a request, and what happens to mail you send us, is set out in the site's own privacy notice.

03 — Questions

Ask, and a person answers.

Email apps@agentsee.work or open an issue. The same policy is kept with the app's source as PRIVACY.md, where its history is public.
